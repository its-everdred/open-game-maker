# Showcase art

These are the studio prototype's showcase scenes and their artwork. The pixel
sprites are from Kenney's CC0 packs; the Goblin Cellar images are captures of
the project's prototype. `showcase-scenes.js` in the games route is copied from `prototypes/studio-v0`,
with image URLs scoped to `/proto/` and formatting and lint-only edits. Keep
that copy in sync with the prototype when changing its scenes.
