Navigation model
One corner

Top right on every screen: resources chip, then the menu orb. Logo top left goes home. Nothing else is fixed chrome.

One menu

The orb opens one sheet: the game's controls on top when in a game, then Needs you, Work, Resources, and a short list of places.

Agents show themselves

While agents work, a banner sits under the corner; tap opens Activity. Play view hides it.

One settings page

Sound, display, controls, voice, account, resources, credits on one scroll. Admin is a place in the menu for admins only.
